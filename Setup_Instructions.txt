Setup and Deployment Instructions

1. Import the Workflow: Upload AI_Product_Video_Generator.json to your n8n instance.
2. Connect Credentials:
   a. Shopify: Add an access token for your store.
   b. AI Services: Add API keys for the image analysis and video generation nodes.
   c. Social Posting Tool: Add your API key to publish to social networks.
   d. Google Sheets: Connect OAuth2 to log video links.
   e. Gmail: Connect OAuth2 to send error alerts.
3. Configuration:
   a. Open the Store settings node and enter your store URL, email, and preferred video style.
   b. Create a Google Sheet with columns: date, product, network, status, link, and note. Update the Google Sheets nodes with this document ID.
